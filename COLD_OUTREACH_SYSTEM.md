### Primary Beachhead: Independent B2B Consultants (6–18 months in)
**Role:** Solo consultant or boutique firm owner (1–3 people), services in ops, HR, finance, marketing, or RevOps  
**Company size:** Self-employed or <5 headcount, billing $5K–$25K/month per client  
**Trigger signals:**
- LinkedIn bio says "consultant," "advisor," or "fractional" + no enterprise employer listed
- Posting 0–2x/month or not at all (check their activity tab)

- Runs a newsletter or mentions "pipeline" problems in posts

**Why they pay:** One new client covers 6–18 months of Spur Pro. LinkedIn is already their stated channel. They have no content team and no budget for an agency. The pain is immediate and the ROI math is obvious. Willingness to pay is high because this is revenue, not a cost.
